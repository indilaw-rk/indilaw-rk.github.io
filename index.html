Useful starting points.

These summaries are general information, not a determination of your rights or the merits of any particular claim.

TOPIC 01

After an injury

Consider noting what happened, when it occurred, who was present, and what records may help explain the event.

TOPIC 02

Accident information

Incident reports, photographs, correspondence, and insurance communications may be relevant to a later discussion.

TOPIC 03

Property and safety

For an incident at a property, details about the location, conditions, timing, and any available documentation may be useful.

TOPIC 04

Organizing records

Keep relevant dates, expenses, reports, and communications together so that you can refer to them accurately.

TOPIC 05

Preparing questions

Write down your main questions and a short timeline before speaking with a legal professional.

TOPIC 06

Understanding next steps

Ask about the professional's experience, the scope of any services offered, applicable deadlines, and communication practices.
